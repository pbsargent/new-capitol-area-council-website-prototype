Cub Scouts

A family-centered first step into Scouting where kids make friends, try hands-on adventures, explore outdoors, and learn to do their best with parents and caring adults beside them.

Find a PackLocal Cub Scout Info

Quick Read

Who it servesYouth in kindergarten through fifth grade.
How it worksDens meet by grade, and packs bring families together for larger activities.
Best promiseFamily, fun, friends, discovery, and confidence.

What Families Should Understand Fast

Cub Scouts should feel easy to enter. A new parent should know the grade path, the meeting rhythm, the first event, and the small way they can help.

Grade-Level Adventures

Each grade has age-appropriate adventures tied to rank progress, character, citizenship, leadership, and personal fitness.

Family Activities

Pack meetings, Pinewood Derby, camping, service, games, and council events give families shared moments beyond a weekly meeting.

Shared Volunteering

The local message that every adult helps in some way should become a clear parent menu, not a vague ask.

Program Planning Lens

Plan Cub Scouts as a first-90-days experience, not just a registration event.

Welcome

Send a simple parent guide with uniform, cost, calendar, Scoutbook, and who to contact.

Belong

Connect each family to a den and a named adult before the first meeting.

Do

Get every new Scout to one hands-on pack activity within the first month.

Help

Ask parents to choose one small job so volunteering feels normal and manageable.

The Page Should Feel Like A Front Porch

This is the warmest program page. It should reduce parent anxiety, show real kids having fun, and make joining feel like a guided path.
