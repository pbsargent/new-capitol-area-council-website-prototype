Kite & Rocket Fest

A Cub Scout-friendly outdoor STEM event built around flight, motion, family fun, and a first taste of Scouting adventure.

Learn More & Sign UpExplore Cub Scouts

Event Snapshot

WhenSeptember 26, 2026. The CAC Cub Scout Activities page notes registration opens July 1.
WhereSmilin V.
Best audienceCub Scout families, packs, and prospective families looking for a hands-on first event.

Why This Event Matters

This should be more than a calendar listing. It can be a recruiting and retention bridge for elementary families.

STEM You Can Feel

Kites and rockets make science tangible: wind, lift, force, design, testing, and iteration.

Family-Friendly Entry

The event gives prospective families an easy way to experience Scouting without already knowing unit vocabulary.

Pack Momentum

Packs can use it as an early fall shared activity after Join Scouting Nights.

Program Planning Notes

Design the page and event communications around parent clarity.

Prepare

Tell families what to bring, what to wear, expected time commitment, and whether materials are provided.

Welcome

Have a visible new-family check-in and pack connection point.

Do

Make the activity hands-on within the first ten minutes of arrival.

Continue

Send families home with the next pack meeting, join link, and Cub Scout contact.
