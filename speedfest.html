Speedfest

A racing-themed family event that connects Pinewood Derby energy, vehicle displays, demonstrations, and Circuit of the Americas excitement with Scouting fun.

Event Snapshot

WhenNovember 14, 2026 on the CAC homepage and Cub Scout Activities page.
WhereCircuit of the Americas.
WhatRacing-themed fun, Pinewood Derby energy, vehicle displays, demonstrations, and family activities.

Why This Event Works

Speedfest has a clear hook: families understand racing, motion, cars, trucks, and spectacle. The page should turn that attention into Scouting connection.

Visible Fun

Pinewood Derby, race car displays, demonstrations, and vehicle experiences are easy to understand from a single glance.

Recruiting Energy

Because it is public-facing and family-friendly, Speedfest can serve as a late-fall membership touchpoint.

Partner Value

The event naturally supports sponsors, community partners, first responders, and activity providers.

Program Planning Notes

The event page should separate free core experiences from activities that may require fees or age/height/weight restrictions.

Clarify

Show date, time, location, parking, cost, and registration status at the top.

Segment

Label activities for Cub Scouts, older youth, families, and prospective members.

Convert

Place “new to Scouting?” help and unit-finder prompts throughout the page.

Partner

Make sponsorship and volunteer opportunities visible without distracting families.
