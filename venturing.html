Venturing

A youth-led program where older teens build adventures with friends, choose activities that matter to them, and practice leadership, planning, communication, and responsibility.

Find a CrewNational Program

Quick Read

Who it servesCoed youth ages 14-20, or 13 and completed eighth grade in many cases.
How it worksCrews choose their own focus and are guided by adult advisors.
Best promiseYouth-led adventure, teamwork, purpose, and flexible belonging.

Venturing Is Not Older Scouts BSA

The page should give Venturing its own identity: flexible, friend-driven, youth-inspired, and built around bigger experiences.

Choose The Adventure

Crews might paddle, travel, climb, serve, build, perform, or explore a special interest. The program adapts to the youth.

Lead With Friends

Venturers plan, organize, communicate, and make decisions together under adult advisor support.

Room To Start

This page should invite teens to join a crew, bring friends, or explore starting a new crew around an interest.

Program Planning Lens

Venturing recruitment works best when the council shows sample crew lives, not just the abstract program.

Imagine

Show examples: climbing crew, service crew, travel crew, STEM crew, outdoor crew.

Gather

Invite teens to bring friends and identify the kind of challenge they want.

Plan

Give crews a simple annual planning model and advisor support.

Launch

Connect interested youth to a crew visit or a council-facilitated startup conversation.

The Page Should Feel Youth-Led

Venturing should sound like an invitation to build something with friends, not like a continuation form for another program.
