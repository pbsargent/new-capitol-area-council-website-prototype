Welcome to the crew

What is Venturing?

A Venturing crew is a group of young people who decide what they want their program to become. Crews can center on outdoor adventure, sports, arts and hobbies, faith, aquatics, service, or a mix chosen by their members.

Who can join? Venturing is open to young people ages 14–20. Youth age 13 may join after completing eighth grade. Previous Scouting experience is not required.

How a crew works

  • Youth officers: Venturers choose leaders and shape the crew’s calendar
  • Adult advisors: trained adults coach, mentor, and support safe activities
  • Shared interests: every crew can build a different identity and program
  • Real responsibility: members plan budgets, logistics, communication, and events

Common questions

See how Venturing fits.

Do I need to have been in Scouts BSA?

No. Venturing welcomes eligible young people with or without previous Scouting experience.

Can I also stay in Scouts BSA?

Yes. Eligible youth may be registered in both programs. A qualified Venturer who earned First Class in Scouts BSA may continue working toward Star, Life, and Eagle before age 18.

What do crews actually do?

It depends on the crew. Capitol Area Council describes local specialties that can include outdoor activities, sports, arts and hobbies, religious life, and aquatics, along with service projects and friendly competition.

What does it cost?

Costs vary by crew and chosen activities. Ask a crew about current membership, dues, equipment, and trip costs before joining. Youth-led budgeting and fundraising can be part of planning bigger adventures.
