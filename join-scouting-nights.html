Event Snapshot

WhenFall recruiting season, with school/community dates listed by the council.
WhoPrimarily new Cub Scout families in grades K-5.
PurposeMatch families to a local pack and make registration feel supported.

The Conversion Moment

Join nights are where marketing becomes membership. The page should answer parent questions before they arrive.

Before You Go

Explain what the event is, how long it takes, whether kids should attend, and what information families need.

At The Event

Families should meet pack leaders, learn costs and calendar basics, and understand parent volunteer expectations.

After You Join

The site should immediately point to first meeting, uniform/shop guidance, financial help, and the next pack activity.

Program Planning Notes

The first 30 days after a join night matter as much as the sign-up itself.

Capture

Track every interested family, even if they do not register that night.

Connect

Assign a pack contact and send a welcome message within 24 hours.

Orient

Send a plain-language new-family guide with calendar, cost, uniform, and volunteer roles.

Retain

Invite families to a near-term event like Kite & Rocket Fest or a pack meeting.
