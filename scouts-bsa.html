Scouts BSA

The classic Scouting experience for youth who are ready to camp, hike, serve, lead, earn merit badges, and grow toward independence with a youth-led troop.

Quick Read

Who it servesYouth ages 11-17, with troops for boys and troops for girls.
How it worksScouts lead meetings and activities with adult guidance.
Best promiseOutdoor skill, service, leadership, challenge, and confidence.

What Youth And Parents Should See

This page should feel more mature than Cub Scouts. It should show youth becoming capable, not just kids joining an activity.

Adventure With Purpose

Camping, hiking, paddling, service, summer camp, and high adventure become the setting for leadership and personal growth.

Merit Badges And Eagle

Merit badges help Scouts explore hobbies, trades, science, business, and future careers while advancement provides a long-term path.

Youth-Led Troops

Parents need to understand the shift: adults guide, but Scouts increasingly plan, decide, and lead.

Program Planning Lens

The goal is a troop visit that feels welcoming and a first campout that feels achievable.

Visit

Make the troop visit easy: date, location, what to wear, who greets the family.

Orient

Explain patrols, youth leadership, advancement, camping gear, and parent roles simply.

Camp

Get new Scouts outdoors quickly with a manageable first trip and clear gear support.

Lead

Show the path from participant to patrol leader, service project, and Eagle candidate.

The Page Should Feel Capable

Scouts BSA should look like a real next step: a place to test limits, learn practical skills, and become trusted with responsibility.
