Welcome aboard

What is a Sea Scout ship?

A ship is a local group of Sea Scouts and trained adult advisors. Young people elect their own officers, choose their adventures, and learn by doing—on lakes, rivers, bays, and wherever their program takes them.

Who can join? Sea Scouts is open to young people ages 14–20, or age 13 after completing eighth grade. You do not need previous Scouting or boating experience.

Aboard your ship
  • Choose the adventures you want to pursue
  • Practice seamanship and water-safety skills
  • Lead meetings, outings, and service projects
  • Belong to a crew that depends on one another
Build your own adventure

In, on, around, and under the water

Every ship builds a different program. The common thread is hands-on adventure with real responsibility.

01

Get on the water

Sail, paddle, cruise, race, fish, dive, or explore—depending on your ship, your interests, and the waters nearby.

02

Build real skills

Learn navigation, boat handling, knots, weather, maintenance, emergency readiness, and responsible seamanship.

03

Lead the crew

Youth officers plan the program and lead the ship, supported by adults who coach, guide, and keep activities safe.

04

Serve together

Protect waterways, support community events, help other Scouts, and put leadership into action through service.

Your year aboard

From first meeting to open water

You will grow one experience at a time. A typical year might look like this:

  1. MeetJoin your shipmates

    Visit a meeting, learn how the ship works, and discover where you fit.

  2. TrainPractice the essentials

    Build confidence with safety, knots, navigation, and boat-handling skills.

  3. ExploreGet underway

    Put those skills to work during day sails, paddling trips, and weekends afloat.

  4. LeadTake responsibility

    Plan an activity, serve as a ship officer, and help your crew succeed.

  5. Go fartherBuild the next voyage

    Choose a cruise, academy, advanced training, or a new stretch goal together.

Skills that stay with you

Chart your own course

Sea Scout advancement recognizes what you learn, how you lead, and the responsibility you take for your ship. Along the way, you can pursue practical training and certifications that support recreation, service, and maritime careers.
