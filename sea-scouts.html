Sea Scouts

A maritime Scouting program where young people learn boating, seamanship, water safety, service, and leadership while making lifelong memories on and around the water.

Find a ShipNational Program

Quick Read

Who it servesCoed youth ages 14-20.
How it worksSea Scout units are called ships and are led by youth officers with adult advisors.
Best promiseWater adventure, technical skill, seamanship, safety, and belonging.

A Distinctive Program Needs A Distinctive Page

Sea Scouts should be visually and verbally unmistakable. The page should help families understand what a ship is and why water-based Scouting matters.

Hands-On Seamanship

Sailing, paddling, navigation, boating safety, lifesaving, CPR, and watercraft skills can become visible proof of growth.

Leadership On The Water

Youth officers plan and conduct activities, learning teamwork where preparation and communication matter.

Adventure And Career Spark

For some youth, Sea Scouts is outdoor adventure. For others, it opens interest in maritime, emergency response, or environmental careers.

Program Planning Lens

Sea Scouts needs concrete local visibility because many families do not already know the program exists.

Explain

Define ship, skipper, boatswain, and the meeting rhythm in plain language.

Show

Use strong water photography and examples of local outings or skills.

Invite

Offer a “come aboard” visit path that feels lower-friction than registering immediately.

Partner

Make room for marinas, boating groups, conservation partners, and maritime mentors.

The Page Should Feel Like An Invitation Aboard

Sea Scouts should look like a specialized adventure program with real skill, real responsibility, and a clear local entry point.
