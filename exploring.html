Exploring

A career-discovery program connecting young people with local professionals, businesses, agencies, and organizations for hands-on experiences that help them discover their future.

Quick Read

Who it servesExploring clubs serve ages 10-14; posts serve ages 14-20.
How it worksPartner organizations host career-specific clubs or posts.
Best promiseCareer clarity, mentorship, leadership, and real-world confidence.

A Program Page And A Partner Page

Exploring has two audiences: youth/families looking for opportunity and organizations that could host a post or club. The page should serve both.

For Youth

Step out of the classroom and into real work environments to test interests, build skills, and meet mentors.

For Partners

Businesses, agencies, and organizations can create hands-on programs that strengthen the community and develop future talent.

Career Fields

Exploring can support law enforcement, fire/EMS, aviation, health care, business, skilled trades, technology, communications, and more.

Program Planning Lens

Exploring growth depends on treating partner development as a membership strategy.

Map

Identify priority career fields and target partners across Central Texas.

Pitch

Give partners a simple “start a post” explanation, sample calendar, and staff contact.

Recruit

Promote opportunities by school, career interest, and age band.

Track

Measure youth participation, partner retention, and career-field demand.

The Page Should Feel Like An Opportunity Portal

Exploring should be crisp, career-facing, and useful to families, schools, and potential partner organizations.
