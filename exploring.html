Step behind the scenes

What is Exploring?

Exploring is a career-education program built through partnerships with businesses, agencies, schools, and community organizations. Participants meet people doing the work now and learn through realistic, age-appropriate activities.

Who can join locally? Capitol Area Council serves coed participants in grades 6–12 and up to age 20. Middle school groups are called clubs; high school and older groups are called posts. Nationally, Exploring clubs serve ages 10–14 and posts serve ages 14–20.

What Explorers gain

  • Career clarity: see what a field is really like before choosing a path
  • Practical experience: learn through demonstrations, scenarios, projects, and site visits
  • Professional connections: meet mentors and begin building a network
  • Leadership: communicate, collaborate, solve problems, and take responsibility

Common questions

Find the right next step.

Do I need to know which career I want?

No. Exploring is designed to help you discover possibilities. Trying a field can confirm an interest, reveal a new one, or help you decide that another direction fits better.

What happens at a meeting?

Activities vary by partner and career field. Meetings may include demonstrations, role-based scenarios, hands-on projects, facility tours, skill practice, guest speakers, and service.

How do I find current local options?

Use Capitol Area Council’s Exploring page. It lists active local programs and the latest meeting and advisor details.

Can my organization start a post?

Yes. Businesses, agencies, schools, and community organizations can partner with the council to connect professionals with interested students. The council helps with program setup, training, safety, and administration.
